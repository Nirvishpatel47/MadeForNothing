About

MADE FOR NOTHING

An evolving archive of questions, research, experiments, writing, books, and things worth understanding.

Welcome to MADE FOR NOTHING. This is an independent, deliberate inquiry space exploring computing systems, software architecture, technical research, and philosophical reflections.

Archive Structure

The archive is categorized across foundational sections:

  • Writing: Thoughtful essays on technology, systems craft, and philosophy.
  • Research: In-depth technical explorations, literature synthesis, and empirical studies.
  • Experiments: Hypotheses, building prototypes, and testing ideas in the real world.
  • Projects: Software architectures, tools, and open-source artifacts.
  • Books: Comprehensive long-form distillations and commentary.
  • Courses: Structured technical curricula, video lectures, marginalia, and Q&As.
